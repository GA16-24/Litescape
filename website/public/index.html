Share it with the server. Or just with Alice.

Click Share on a placement and players with the mod can follow along. Choose everyone, a list of players, or only you, and the server enforces it.

When someone shares with you, you get a request. Accept it or don't; the server remembers either way. Only the owner or an op can unshare.

Build it in survival order.

Order mode walks the schematic one stage at a time, with the materials for each stage. Water waits for whatever holds it, and lava goes last so nobody makes obsidian by accident.

It doesn't plan scaffolding or walking routes. You're on your own up there.

Find out who actually built it.

Contributors lists who placed how many matching blocks, with their Minecraft face. Buckets, pistons and /fill don't count. We checked for cheaters.

The rest of the inventory

Load in Litematica
One click drops a shared schematic into your game at its coordinates, chests and hoppers included.
Surroundings
Snapshot 8, 16 or 32 blocks of land around the build so it doesn't float in the void.
Mob spawning
Marks every block in view a zombie could spawn on at night. Spawnproof the farm before the zombies find it.
Shader packs
Your Iris packs, compiled by the mod and run in WebGL 2. A browser isn't a GPU, so it's terrain only. Complementary, BSL, Sildur's and Solas work; Photon runs, but its sky comes out black.
Your textures
The model uses your resource pack, so the cottage looks like your cottage.
The small stuff
Layer slicing, click to isolate a material, screenshots, a command palette, a switcher that remembers every server you've joined, light and dark, and 10 languages.

Install it without opening mods/.

  1. Get the app

    It finds your Minecraft Launcher, Prism, Modrinth App and CurseForge instances.

    On a Mac, brew install --cask GA16-24/tap/litescape skips the notarization fuss.

  2. Click Install

    It adds the mod, plus Fabric API, MaLiLib and Litematica if they're missing.

  3. Type /litweb

    Or click the link in chat. The viewer opens at 127.0.0.1:8723.

Server owners: it's optional, and suspicious by default.

Without the mod on the server, players see whatever chunks they have loaded. Add the same jar and Fabric API to the server's mods/ and shares sync. The server only reads loaded chunks, so nobody can stall it with a far-away selection. Set shared-progress=ops if you worry about scouting.

The viewer listens on 127.0.0.1 and rejects any other origin, so other websites can't read your world.
